## 2. Platform Role as Discovery & Curator Engine
SMILEORA functions strictly as an entertainment discovery engine, metadata catalog, and authorized streaming directory:
1. **Zero Pirated Hosting**: SMILEORA does not host, upload, or store unauthorized full-length commercial anime videos on its primary servers.
2. **Authorized Embedding**: Any video playback embedded directly uses official publicly available partner frames (such as official studio trailers and licensed distributor channels).
3. **Outbound Authorized Links**: When an anime is available on official legal streaming platforms (e.g., Crunchyroll, Netflix, Bilibili), SMILEORA prominently links visitors to those legal destinations to support original anime creators and production staff.
